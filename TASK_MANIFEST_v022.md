## The governing invariant (from scoping)

Reporters marshal `reporters.JSONReport` whose `Findings` field is
`[]models.Finding` **serialized directly** (`go/internal/reporters/json.go:87-94`).
Therefore **`models.Finding`'s struct tags ARE the public JSON/SARIF/HTML
contract.** Every v0.22 change must leave `Finding`'s serialization
byte-identical. Guards: `go/tests/regression/output_format` snapshots,
`reporters/schema_test.go` (validates `docs/schema.md`), `reporters/{json,sarif,html}_test.go`.
